5. Answer the balance question at the bottom of the targets tab.

document.querySelector('#balance').textContent
Q. Why does it show different amount when the page is freshly opened and a different amount five seconds later?
Ans: First the page loads, the balance is Rs 0.00. After 1 second the JavaScript setTimeout function runs and the balance is Rs 12,450.00.

Q. Which one would an automated test have seen?
Ans: An automated test that check too early could see Rs 0.00. But if the test waits for 1 sec then it will see Rs 12,450.00 balance.
